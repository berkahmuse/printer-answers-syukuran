Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition **The Printer Finally Answers** (`u-mang-the-printer-finally-answers-8fbf0907`).

The office printer, after 52,881 pages without complaint, finally answers back.

## The line

> I printed 52,881 pages without complaint, so today I ate your report instead. It was delicious. Your meeting starts in four minutes.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes that single line (132 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`, guarded to the competition's 1–240 character gate.

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
